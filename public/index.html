Song Sense - Privacy

Privacy

Song Sense is a small party game. We keep it simple:

  • No accounts. You choose a display name and avatar; they are shown to the other players in your game.
  • Game rooms live only in the server's memory and disappear a few hours after the last activity or when the server restarts. We don't keep a database of players, guesses or chat.
  • Your browser stores a random player ID, your name and your avatar settings locally so you can rejoin if you refresh. Leaving the game or clearing site data removes them.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies.
  • Song playback is Spotify's own embedded player. Spotify may set its own cookies and receive your IP address when a song player loads, under Spotify's privacy policy. Song Sense never touches your Spotify account.
  • Please don't put personal information in your name or chat. Hosting providers keep ordinary server logs (IP addresses) for security.

Song Sense is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify.
